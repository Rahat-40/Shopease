 ShopEase - Smart Sell & Buy Platform

ShopEase is a comprehensive e-commerce platform designed to bridge the gap between buyers and sellers. It features a robust administration system, interactive dashboards for participants, and a seamless shopping experience.

 Key Features

 Buyer Dashboard:
 Product Exploration: Browse and search through a wide catalog of products.
 Wishlist & Cart: Save items for later or manage your current shopping basket.
 Order Management: Securely place orders and track their delivery status.
 User Support: Direct communication with administrators for assistance.

Seller Dashboard:
 Product Management: Add, update, and manage your inventory with ease.
 Inventory Tracking: Monitor stock levels and product visibility.
 Order Fulfillment: View and update the status of customer orders specifically for your products.

Admin Dashboard:
 Platform Management: Oversee all users (Buyers/Sellers) and product listings.
 Order Overview: Monitor all platform-wide transactions.
 Support System: Real-time message threads to provide support to users.
 Statistics: Access high-level stats about platform performance.
